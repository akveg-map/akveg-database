In Alaska and northwestern Canada, field data on vegetation composition and structure remain fragmented across jurisdictional boundaries. To address this gap, we developed the Alaska Vegetation Database (AKVEG): a harmonized, relational database of plot-level vegetation observations from 1975 to 2025. This repository contains the compiled data tables from v2.11.0 of the public database, exported as 16 comma-separated values (.csv) tables.

To build AKVEG, we integrated data from 100 survey and monitoring projects across Alaska and adjacent Canada. Using a  reproducible scripting workflow, we harmonized measurement units, documented methodological metadata, and reconciled field identifications to a taxonomic standard.

The AKVEG Database comprises 701,216 records of foliar cover for vascular plants, bryophytes, and lichens from 44,324 unique site visits. The 16 tables in this deposit are organized into three categories: 
- Data Tables (13 tables): Core observational data and associated metadata, including methodological metadata on each project, site, and site visit, and associated field observations.
- Documentation Tables (2 tables): Schema metadata, including a data dictionary and attributes for each field.
- Taxonomy Tables (1 table): A synonymized regional checklist of plant and lichen names that maps taxon concepts to currently accepted names and taxonomic authorities.

Spanning five decades, four bioclimatic zones, and two nations, this harmonized dataset supports vegetation mapping, community classification, and the modeling of plant community responses to environmental change.
